# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

macOS (Apple Silicon)

## Users

Founders, engineering leaders, managers, consultants, and knowledge workers who attend 10+ meetings a week and need an executive-grade second brain that connects discussions, tracks commitments, and provides pre-meeting briefings without manual note-taking overhead.

## Product Purpose

Your meetings are not isolated events. They are connected by people, projects, and themes. Pluto is a local-first, open-source desktop second brain that captures meeting audio, transcribes locally, resolves entities into a continuous knowledge graph, and proactively keeps teams accountable.

## Positioning & Differentiators

Unlike traditional meeting note-takers (such as Granola or Otter) that generate dead, isolated meeting documents that sit forgotten in folders, Pluto turns conversations into an interconnected, living knowledge system:
- **Pre-Meeting Intelligence**: Delivers executive briefings 30 minutes before your calls with attendee histories, past commitments, and open decisions.
- **Live Documents**: Automatically updates rolling project spaces, 1-on-1 relationship timelines, and team standup trackers after every meeting.
- **Proactive Accountability Engine**: Tracks the full lifecycle of commitments (Active, Due, Overdue, Stale) with smart due dates and macOS reminders.
- **Invisible, Bot-Free Capture**: Listens directly via system audio with on-device Voice ID. Zero awkward meeting bots joining your calls.
- **100% Private & Open Source**: Built for Apple Silicon. On-device speech transcription runs locally. Supports Ollama for 100% offline inference, with optional OpenRouter for BYOK cloud models. Audio never leaves the machine.

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone**: Warm, sophisticated, executive, quiet authority, trustworthy.
- **Visuals**: Inspired by a curated private study. Deep Midnight Blue (`#1A2340`), Champagne Gold (`#D4B483`), Warm Cream (`#FAF8F5`), Pure White elevated cards with deep, soft shadows.
- **Anti-patterns Refused**: No sterile enterprise SaaS clichés, no fake UI screenshots, no generic card soup, no dark-glow halos.
