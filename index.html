Your models. Your terms.

Pluto processes audio transcripts 100% locally on your Mac by default with Ollama. If you prefer cloud intelligence, bring your own API key to connect OpenRouter with complete autonomy.

Local with Ollama

On-device speech transcription and local model inference execute on your Apple Silicon hardware. Audio recordings, transcripts, and notes never leave your SSD.

Cloud with OpenRouter

Want cloud speed or running on lighter hardware? Bring your personal OpenRouter API key to access Claude, GPT, or custom models with zero markup.

Open Source & Auditable

Inspect every line of code on GitHub. Transcripts live in a standard local SQLite database that you own. Zero telemetry, no hidden trackers, and no lock-in.
